Featured work

Ledgerly: bank statements in, reconciled books out.

A public rewrite of an internal tool I built for my own work, made from scratch with invented data so anyone can try it. No login, nothing to install.

{% include "partials/logo.html" %} ledgerly / bank statements Open demo →
  1. 1

    Reads the bank's PDF statements

    And checks that each one adds up: opening balance plus every line must equal the closing balance. If a line can't be read, posting stops.

  2. 2

    Suggests how to book every line

    Payroll goes to wages, a customer payment clears an invoice. Lines it doesn't recognise wait for a person to decide.

  3. 3

    Explains every difference in plain English

    The books are compared with the bank line by line. A payment booked twice or two swapped digits get found, explained and fixed with a correcting entry.

Five minutes is enough: open a statement PDF, post one to the ledger, fix the four planted bookkeeping mistakes, then switch off the data warehouse and see what happens.
