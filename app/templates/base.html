Welcome to Ledgerly

A small finance back-office tool. Every company has to prove that its books match its bank account. Ledgerly does the boring part.

  1. Bank statements arrive as PDFs. Ledgerly reads them and suggests how to book every line.
  2. You approve the bookings - one click sends them to the ledger.
  3. Reconciliation compares bank vs. books and explains every difference in plain English.
  4. Accruals close the month - costs not yet invoiced are estimated, booked and settled later.

The bank, the company and every number are made up. Nothing you do here is saved - feel free to break things.
