How the month-end close works

  1. Statements arrive

    Every working day the bank sends a PDF. Three are waiting for you, and one more arrives while you click around.

  2. Ledgerly reads the PDF

    Each line gets a suggested account - "Payroll" goes to wages, "Payment from…" clears a customer invoice.

  3. You approve and post

    One click sends the entries to the ledger. Unusual lines wait for a human decision.

  4. Reconcile

    The ledger is compared with the bank line by line; every difference is explained and can be fixed.

  5. Close the accruals

    Costs used but not invoiced yet still belong to this month. Settle last month's estimates, book new ones, sign off. Open accruals →
